Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up

How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
